Who We Are

Established in 2008, Obusubiri MM & Associates is a professional Audit, Tax, Financial Advisory, Accounting and Capacity Building firm serving clients across Kenya and the wider African region.

The firm has developed strong expertise in tax compliance, tax objections, tax appeals before the Tax Appeals Tribunal, tax exemption applications, audit and assurance services, helping organisations navigate complex regulatory and compliance requirements.

We work with NGOs, Development Partners, Government Institutions, Faith-Based Organisations, Educational Institutions, SACCOs and Private Sector Organisations.
